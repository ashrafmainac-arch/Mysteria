The Great Pyramid of Giza

📍 Location: Giza, Egypt

📅 Built Around: 2560 BCE

⭐ Mystery Level: ★★★★★

About the Mystery

The Great Pyramid of Giza is one of the most extraordinary structures ever built by humans. Constructed more than 4,500 years ago, it continues to amaze historians, engineers, and archaeologists. Built from approximately 2.3 million stone blocks, the pyramid was created with remarkable precision despite the limited technology of the time.

Even today, experts continue to debate how such enormous stones were transported, lifted, and placed so accurately. Its near-perfect alignment with the cardinal directions and its incredible engineering have made it one of history's greatest unsolved mysteries.

Unsolved Questions

  • How were millions of heavy stones transported?
  • How was the pyramid aligned so accurately?
  • Were unknown construction techniques used?

🏺 Did You Know?

The Great Pyramid of Giza is so accurately aligned that each of its four sides points almost exactly to the north, south, east and west. Even today, this level of precision is considered an amazing engineering achievement.
