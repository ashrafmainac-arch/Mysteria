The Roswell Incident

📍 Location: Roswell, New Mexico, USA

📅 Year: 1947

⭐ Mystery Level: ★★★★★

About the Mystery

In July 1947, an unusual object crashed near Roswell, New Mexico. At first, a military statement said that a "flying disc" had been recovered. Shortly afterward, the explanation was changed, stating that the debris came from a weather balloon.

Over the years, many people have believed the object may have been something extraordinary, while others accept the official explanations. The event remains one of the most discussed UFO incidents in history and continues to inspire books, documentaries, and investigations.

Questions That Remain

  • What actually crashed near Roswell?
  • Why did the first military statement mention a "flying disc"?
  • Why is the incident still debated today?

👽 Did You Know?

Decades after the incident, the U.S. government explained that the debris was related to a classified military project. Even so, the Roswell Incident continues to be debated and remains one of the world's most famous UFO mysteries.
